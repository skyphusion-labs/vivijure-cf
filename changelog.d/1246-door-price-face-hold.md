### feat(modules): every motion door states its price and how well it holds a face

Each motion door now carries `usage.face_hold` (hold, two, one or drift), and every metered door states its cost: `ui.cost_usd_per_second`, or the new `ui.cost_usd_per_clip` for a door billed per clip (infinitetalk, cf-hailuo). Prices are the provider's, with no markup, read 2026-11-05 from the Cloudflare and RunPod model pages cited beside each figure. Newly priced: alibaba-wan, alibaba-wan-lora, cf-flux-3-video, cf-grok-video, cf-hailuo, cf-hh1-r2v, cf-veo, infinitetalk, minimax-hailuo. cf-seedance stays unpriced, because its Cloudflare page lists no price, and its limits now say so. The grades match what the Make a video page computes today, so nothing it shows changes until it reads the field. Closes the manifest half of cf#1246.
