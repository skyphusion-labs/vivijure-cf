### Change: two-person stills default to PRF3 (cf#1281, Conrad's ruling 2026-11-13)

The keyframe module (0.3.11) now defaults to the pose skeleton on (`pose_conditioning` "on") at `pose_scale` 0.3, with per-region plate text on (`plate_regional_text` true) and the standing figure (`pose_template` stand). This is "PRF3", measured best on three of four boards: the right two people on 24 of 24 stills, against 14 of 24 "both" for the old default. The workbench board regresses, with faces looking into the lens (5 of 6 to 3 of 6); that is accepted and tracked in cf#1442.

**Cached stills redraw.** The backend's reuse hash includes the pose scale and the region-text flag on every still, one-person shots included. So a still drawn under the old defaults no longer matches, and the next render that would have reused it draws it again. Measured in the `vivijure` bucket on 2026-11-13 UTC (read-only listing): 681 shots carry a stored reuse hash (444 `*.latest.json` pointers in 76 projects, plus 237 shots with only a legacy `.hash` sidecar). Of those, 2 were drawn with exactly the new values (cf#1281's chess and shake PRF3 runs). So up to 679 stills redraw once, each when its project next renders it; nothing is redrawn by this release itself. A job that sets the old values explicitly keeps the old hashes.
