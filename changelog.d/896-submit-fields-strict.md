### fix(render): a submit door refuses a field it does not read, and a quality tier it does not know

`POST /api/storyboard/render`, `POST /api/storyboard/render-from-keyframes` and `POST /api/render/film` read only the fields they know and silently dropped the rest. A request with `quality_tier: "draft"` (the field is `qualityTier`) or a top-level `resolution` (it belongs in the motion config) got a 201 and a render at `final` and at the motion door's default size. A `qualityTier` that was not a known tier also ran at `final`. Each door now answers `400` before any spend, naming every field it does not read, with a hint for the common ones, and refuses an unknown `qualityTier`. An absent `qualityTier` is still `final`. The field lists are held equal to each handler's body type at compile time. The check runs only on outside requests: a retry replay and the parts of a long film are built by the studio and pass as before (vivijure-cf#896).
