### feat(panel): a one-screen Make a video page for first-time creators

A new page at `/make` takes someone from "a short video with me and my friend in it" to a finished video on one screen: pick or add up to 4 people (a name and one selfie runs the existing cast steps, with progress in plain words), write the idea, pick Vertical (the default), Square or Wide, pick Draft, Good or Best, see one total, press Make my video, confirm, then watch a progress bar and get a player with Download and Edit in advanced planner. The plan is saved to its own project. The video style is chosen for face consistency first, not price (`public/make-style.js`, `docs/make-a-video.md`), and a shape no consistent style can make is not offered. A missing price reads "price unknown". The page uses only routes the planner and cast page already use. A new `PANEL_HOME` setting puts the page at `/` and first in the nav, with the planner kept as Advanced; unset keeps today's behaviour, and `GET /api/whoami` carries `panel_home` only when it is set.
