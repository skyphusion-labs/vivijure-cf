### feat(films): review a part's shots and make a new take

Each part on the film board opens a shot list: still, seconds, prompt, line and error, with the clip on a tap. A new take can change the prompt, line or voice, and a waiting take can be cancelled. "Go to shot number" opens the right part. The board warns when the scheduler has stopped. It says when a step takes effect, since the cron acts on it within about a minute.
